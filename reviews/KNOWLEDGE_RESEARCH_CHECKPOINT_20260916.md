## Source/provenance progress

`knowledge/source_registry.v1.json` now separates:

- `FIRST_PARTY`;
- `PRIMARY_CANDIDATE`;
- `REFERENCE_ONLY`;
- `CANDIDATE_QUARANTINE`;
- `REJECT_MVP`.

Important source findings now encoded:

- CEFR-J Wordlist 1.6 is the intended A1–B1 vocabulary-level prior, but its reported checksum/size remain `observed_external_not_lingua_verified` until this project independently fetches and hashes the official artifact;
- Open English WordNet 2025 is a CC BY 4.0 candidate for sense/definition/semantic evidence, but no first-synset-wins behavior is allowed;
- PanLex CC0 is a translation-equivalence candidate, not an automatic Vietnamese definition source;
- NGSL is useful as frequency/usefulness evidence but remains quarantined because current list distribution is CC BY-SA 4.0;
- `ipa-dict` is explicitly source-lineage sensitive: repository-level MIT does not erase upstream per-language licences;
- CEFR sentence-profile research is reference-only and informs a multi-signal text-difficulty model rather than a sentence-length or word-level threshold.
